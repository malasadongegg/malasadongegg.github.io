Hi, I'm Mark.

BACHELOR OF SCIENCE IN INFORMATION TECHNOLOGY · 4TH YEAR

I build small, practical software end to end — usually solo, usually because I wanted the thing to exist. Lately that's meant an AI usage tracker people can embed on their profile, and a personal finance app I run on my own phone every day. I like tools that respect your data: local-first where possible, honest about what's estimated versus measured, and free of dark patterns.

Most of what I build falls into one of two buckets: school activities, or something I actually needed in my own day-to-day life. Both show up below — each project is tagged with which kind it is.

Self-taught, Windows-based, and comfortable across the stack — from SQL migrations and financial-engine logic up through React UI. I test on real devices instead of just simulators, and I'd rather ship something small and correct than something big and half-working.

“If you are tired, do it tired.”
